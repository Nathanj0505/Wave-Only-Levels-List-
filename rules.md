# Rules

## Requirements
- Must be playable on 60/120/240 FPS.
- Must be decorated enough to not be considered a layout.
- Must be 30+ seconds.

## Submission Format
- Video link required.
- FPS must be shown.
- Level ID must be included.
  
## Verification
- Raw footage may be requested.
- No hacks, macros, or speed hack.
- Must show full run.
- Click sounds must be included in video.
